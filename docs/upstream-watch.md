# Upstream watch

The sources the Hex and FluidVoice watch follows every two days, and what was
last reviewed in each. Each run reviews only what is new since that point and
records its decisions in `activities/`.

Licences decide what may be reused. MIT, Apache or BSD code may be adapted,
credited in `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`. For GPL, AGPL, unknown or closed
sources, only the behaviour may be described and rebuilt in our own words
(clean room, see `CONTRIBUTING.md`). Nothing is used for model training.

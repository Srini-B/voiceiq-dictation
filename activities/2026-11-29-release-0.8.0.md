# 2026-11-29: Release 0.8.0 (94)

## Why 0.8.0

Versions follow semantic versioning from this release on (AGENTS.md). This one is a minor release, not 0.7.19. It replaces Gemini with AssemblyAI and asks users for a new key, adds draft-then-written dictation on Mac and iPhone, and adds meeting waveforms and a playback bar. Build 94 follows 0.7.18 (93), which shipped from the release-0.7.18-gemini branch.

## What ships

Everything on main since 0.7.17 (92):

- **Provider:** AssemblyAI in place of Gemini for dictation, meetings and notes (#121 and follow-ups), a Language setting, and the key check from Blurt (#144).
- **Dictation:** draft then written text on Mac (#130) and iPhone (#160, #171: Messages, Reminders, Contacts and Calendar). The writer no longer echoes text that was already before the caret (#147). Mac fixes for the draft swap (#160, #171).
- **Meetings:** a recap that restates tasks already taken (#145), notes that follow the language setting, and screen names read from the browser holding the call (#146). Raw speaker labels kept out of notes (#165). Live and playback waveforms with seek and speed (#149, #162). The player pauses on a call or Siri on iPhone (#163). Meeting timers count up (#150). Pill and list fixes (#143). The Mac stays awake until a meeting's notes are written (#148).
- **iPhone keyboard:** Adaptive touch (#167), and learning from fixes to dictated text (#168).
- **iPhone:** a dictation whose audio isn't on the phone says so (#170).
- **Fixes:** opening Settings › Advanced on a fresh Mac no longer crashes the app (#169; present since 0.7.14).
- **Test tooling:** test mode, with sync off, its own data folder and its own Keychain keys (#161, #169), the Mac mini app lock and queue (#140), and CI build logs (#159).

The user notice is release-notes/0.8.0.notice.md: the AssemblyAI key, the LLM Gateway balance and the Language setting. It repeats no earlier notice.

## Not tested on a real iPhone

The iPhone drafts (#160, #171), Adaptive touch (#167), learning from dictation fixes (#168), the recorder waveform (#149) and the interruption pause (#163) were checked on the Simulator only. The TestFlight build is their first real-device check. Try these:
- a dictation in Messages, Reminders and Calendar notes, pressing a key as the draft appears;
- a phone call during meeting playback;
- fixing a dictated word, then finding it under Dictionary › Auto-learned.

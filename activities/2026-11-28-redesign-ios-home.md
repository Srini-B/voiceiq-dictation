# 2026-11-28 — Redesign, step 2: iPhone Home holds the history

## Changes

- Home and History are one page. It shows:
  - the large title, a search field, and the setup checklist until setup is done;
  - one line of totals that opens Statistics;
  - every dictation grouped by day, as a hairline list on the white canvas.
- **Rows.** Each row has the time and app, the text (three lines), and the
  length as a chip. The ⋯ menu holds Copy, Share and Delete. Swipe to delete
  still works.
- **Dock.** A dock floats at the bottom. "Try the VoiceiQ keyboard" opens the
  tryout in a sheet. The Talk key records a note and opens Notes; while a note
  records, the key is red and opens it.
- **Mic session.** It shows as a small capsule in the toolbar with the time
  left. Its menu turns it off.
- **Tabs.** The tab bar is Home, Notes and Settings. The History tab is gone.

## Why

This follows the Voicenotes reference: one list page, no cards around rows,
and the record action on a floating dock. The Talk key is VoiceiQ's own: a
blue rounded square that turns red only while recording.

# Keyboard emoji move to Emoji 17.0 (2026-11-05)

**Why.** The keyboard's emoji came from Unicode's Emoji 15.0, the newest set
iOS 17.0 (the deployment target) draws. iPhones on iOS 26.4 draw Emoji 17.0,
so the panel lacked 44 emoji people already see in Apple's keyboard. Emoji
18.0 (2026-09-15) is not expected on iPhone until about iOS 27.4, so it stays
out. Emoji newer than the running iOS show as empty boxes, so each one must
appear only where iOS draws it. Versions per Emojipedia's Apple changelogs:
Emoji 15.1 in iOS 17.4 (5 March 2024), 16.0 in iOS 18.4 (31 March 2025), 17.0
in iOS 26.4 (24 March 2026, which also added skin tones to 🤼 and 👯).

**What changed.**
- `scripts/generate-keyboard-data.py` pins
  https://www.unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/emoji/emoji-test.txt (Unicode moved the
  file at 17.0; `/Public/emoji/17.0/` returns 404). Every emoji newer than 15.0
  is written with its version from that file, `🫩@16.0`, in `emoji.txt`,
  `emoji-words.txt` (emoji and skin tones) and `emoji-shortcuts.txt`.
- A shortcut whose best emoji is newer than 15.0 also lists the next best,
  so "ballet" suggests the ballet dancer on iOS 26.4 and ballet shoes before.
- `EmojiData.drawable` maps the running iOS to the newest version it draws
  and drops newer emoji as each file loads: the panel, search, skin tones (an
  emoji whose tones are too new offers none) and suggestions. Recents keep
  only emoji the keyboard still offers.
- The download cache is keyed by URL, so a moved pin no longer reuses the old
  file.
- Emoji names come from FUTO's `gemoji.json` at `31e720dd`, unchanged; it
  already names all 44 new emoji.

| | Emoji 15.0 and older | 15.1 | 16.0 | 17.0 | Total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Before | 1,870 | | | | 1,870 |
| After | 1,870 | 28 | 8 | 8 | 1,914 |

Plus toned forms: 90 at 15.1 (facing-right people) and 35 single-tone forms
at 17.0 (ballet dancer, 👯 and 🤼 and their men and women forms). Unicode 17.0
also moved 🦀 🦞 🦐 🦑 🦪 from Food & Drink to Animals & Nature and moved 👪,
🎺 and 🪙 within their groups.

**Verified.**
- Regenerated: `lexicon.bin` is byte-identical to HEAD (`cmp`, sha256
  `02dd32db…`).
- With the iOS 17.0 filter applied, `emoji-words.txt` and
  `emoji-shortcuts.txt` equal HEAD's, and the panel has HEAD's 1,870 emoji.
- Scratch run of `EmojiData.swift` on macOS against the generated files, with
  the newest version forced to 15.0, 15.1, 16.0 and 17.0: panel 1,870, 1,898,
  1,906, 1,914; orca in search and as the "orca" suggestion only at 17.0;
  "ballet" suggests ballet shoes below 17.0; 👯 and the ballet dancer offer
  tones only at 17.0, facing-right runners from 15.1; stored recents 🫍, 👯🏽,
  🍋‍🟩, 😀, 🫩 shrink to 😀 at 15.0.
- iPhone 17e Simulator, iOS 27.0 (Debug build, VoiceiQ keyboard in Safari):
  the landslide shows in Travel & Places, searching "orca" finds 🫍, typing
  "ballet" puts the ballet dancer in the third bar slot, picking it adds it
  to recents, and holding it offers five tones.
- Not run on an iOS 17 or 18 Simulator: no runtime older than 27.0 is
  installed, so hiding on older iOS rests on the scratch run.

**Rollback.** Revert the commit, then rerun
`scripts/generate-keyboard-data.py`, or restore the four `Resources` emoji
files from the previous commit.

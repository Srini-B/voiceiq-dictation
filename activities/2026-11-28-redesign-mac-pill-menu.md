# 2026-11-28 — Redesign, step 7: Mac pill and menu bar menu

## Changes

- **The floating pill is dark in both appearances.** Its panel uses the dark
  appearance. Its capsule is the pill ink with a faint white ring, and on
  macOS 26 and later it keeps the glass. A dark capsule reads over any app,
  light or dark.
- **Bars and spinners use the brand blue.** The live bars, the writing sweep,
  the draft spinner and the draft underline drop the Google four colours and
  the "AI shimmer". A meeting's recording dot uses the shared record red.
- **Menu bar menu.**
  - The main actions show outline symbols.
  - Under them, a "Recent — click to copy" group lists the last three
    dictations. Each shows on one line, with the full text in its tooltip.
  - History and Add to Dictionary move below that group.

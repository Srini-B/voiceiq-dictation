# 2026-11-28: Writer prompt fixes for D4 on the LLM Gateway

## Why

Release gate (c): D4 for the shipping writer (GPT-6 Luna, effort none, over
STT-3.7 Realtime with no keyterms) on the real LLM Gateway was 88.7 (Fable
5.2; OpenRouter 85.2). Three cases lost the points every time, all writer
faults: long_02 dropped the speaker's labels ("what went well is") and
turned "people say it drops" into "it drops", and added "should";
clean_email kept a one-paragraph email on one line; clean_field started
". Push" after a field ending "I think we should".

## What changed (`PromptV1`)

- A rule to keep, word for word, the words a speaker uses to say what comes
  next, and claims as framed ("people say …").
- The email layout applies to a one-paragraph message that opens with a
  greeting and a name and ends with a sign-off and a name, with one example
  (names and content unlike any eval case).
- The field rule: text ending in a word that needs more after it (should,
  to, the …) stops mid-sentence, so CLEAN starts lowercase with no
  punctuation, even when RAW starts with a capital.

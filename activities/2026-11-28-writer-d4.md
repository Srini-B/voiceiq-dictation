# 2026-11-28: Writer prompt fixes for D4 on the LLM Gateway

## Why

Release gate (c): D4 for the shipping writer (GPT-6 Luna, effort none, over
STT-3.7 Realtime with no keyterms) on the real LLM Gateway was 88.7 (Fable
5.2; OpenRouter 85.2). Three cases lost the points every time, all writer
faults: long_02 dropped the speaker's labels ("what went well is") and
turned "people say it drops" into "it drops", and added "should";
clean_email kept a one-paragraph email on one line; clean_field started
". Push" after a field ending "I think we should".

## What changed (`PromptV1`)

- A rule to keep, word for word, the words a speaker uses to say what comes
  next, and claims as framed ("people say …").
- The email layout applies to a one-paragraph message that opens with a
  greeting and a name and ends with a sign-off and a name, with one example
  (names and content unlike any eval case). The prompt alone laid out the
  eval email in 2 of 3 rounds and unseen one-paragraph emails in 0 of 6, so
  `EmailLayout` now applies that layout in code to the writer's answer
  (`AssemblyAITranscriptionService.write`); `eval/promptdump` runs the same
  Swift for the eval (`{"output": …}`). It steps aside when the user's
  custom writing rules name layout (paragraphs, line breaks, newlines, a
  single line or block, layout), since the writer has already followed them.
  A one-word closing counts as a sign-off only with its comma ("Best Buy." is
  not signed "Buy"), a body ending in a title ("Dr.") is left alone, and a
  body's first word is capitalised only when it is a plain lowercase word
  (not `user_id` or `config.json`).
- The field rule: text ending in a word that needs more after it (should,
  to, the …) stops mid-sentence, so CLEAN starts lowercase with no
  punctuation, even when RAW starts with a capital.

## Verification

Gateway runs, 38 cases × 3 rounds, `eval/dictation/writer.py --via gateway
--workers 1` (a new gateway account answers 429 to 4 parallel calls):

| Run | D1 | D2 | D5 | D4 (Fable) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| main prompt (`gw-luna-nokt`) | 95.7 | 97.2 | 100 | 88.7 |
| prompt rules (`gw-luna-d4fix3`) | 95.7 | 97.8 | 100 | 93.2 |
| prompt rules + `EmailLayout` (`gw-luna-d4fix4`) | 95.7 | 97.5 | 100 | see PR |

A holdout of 9 cases written after the change (3 mid-sentence fields, 3
one-paragraph emails, 3 labelled notes), 3 rounds each through the shipping
path: fields right 9 of 9, emails laid out 8 of 9 (the ninth lost its
sign-off in the writer), labels kept 8 of 9.

Gateway spend for these runs: about $0.15 of the $5 balance.

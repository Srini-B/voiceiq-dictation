# 2026-11-28 — Redesign, step 8: meetings chrome

Only the frame around meetings changes. Waveforms and playback come in
separate meeting waveform work, and meeting logic is untouched.

- **Mac.**
  - The Meetings list opens with a "Meetings" title, like History.
  - The Record button uses the brand blue. While recording it turns the
    shared record red, which also marks the dot on the recording row.
- **iPhone.**
  - Notes is a plain hairline list on the white canvas, like Home.
  - Its section header is a muted caption. The recorder card keeps the page
    margins.

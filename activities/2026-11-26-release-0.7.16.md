## Release notes

`scripts/generate-release-notes.py` now puts `release-notes/<version>.notice.md` first, word for word. 0.7.16's notice tells users to remove the VoiceiQ Meetings Chrome extension by hand, and that a stored "On device" writing setting is now Off.

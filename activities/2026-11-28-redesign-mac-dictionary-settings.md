# 2026-11-28 — Redesign, step 5: Mac Dictionary and Settings panes

## Changes

- **Dictionary.**
  - The page opens with a title and a one-line explanation, then a capsule
    search.
  - The add form is a white card with a hairline ring.
  - Words are hairline rows, not a grid of grey cards. Each row shows the
    star, the spelling, what it is heard as, and a remove button.
  - The two groups are "Your words" and "Learned from your corrections",
    each with a count.
  - The empty state is one muted line.
- **Settings.** Settings panes sit on the light grey grouped page under their
  white cards. Data panes (History, Meetings, Dictionary, Cost) are on the
  white canvas.

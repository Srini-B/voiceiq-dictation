# 2026-11-28 — Redesign, step 3: iPhone dictation page and Settings

## Changes

- **Dictation page.** A dictation now opens as a page you read. It shows a
  meta line (date, app and length), then the text at reading size. When the
  written text differs from what was heard, a "Written / What you said"
  switch picks between them. Copy is the one dark pill, with Share beside it.
  Retry, the recording and the details follow as plain sections with a
  caption and hairlines, not cards.
- **Settings.** Settings and its pages sit on the light grey grouped
  background. Rows use outline symbols in the secondary ink, with no coloured
  tiles. The version moved from a header to the footer, under the last group.

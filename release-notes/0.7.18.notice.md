- On the Mac: if you loaded the VoiceiQ Meetings extension in Chrome, remove it yourself: open chrome://extensions, find VoiceiQ Meetings, and click Remove. VoiceiQ no longer uses it, and updating the app does not remove it.
- Meetings are now always transcribed by Gemini. If you had set Meetings to On device, your meeting audio now goes to Gemini with your key. The on-device choice named people and placed their words too poorly on real meetings. A meeting recorded while Gemini can't be reached (offline, or no key yet) keeps its audio and is transcribed once Gemini can be reached.

- On the Mac: if you loaded the VoiceiQ Meetings extension in Chrome, remove it yourself: open chrome://extensions, find VoiceiQ Meetings, and click Remove. VoiceiQ no longer uses it, and updating the app does not remove it.
- Gemini is gone. VoiceiQ now uses AssemblyAI for dictation, meetings and notes, and needs an AssemblyAI API key: sign up at assemblyai.com/dashboard/signup (no card, $50 of free credit) and copy your key from assemblyai.com/dashboard/api-keys into Settings → Advanced (on iPhone, Settings › API Key). Your old Gemini key is deleted from the Keychain when this version opens, so going back to 0.7.17 means entering it again.
- Dictation is transcribed while you speak, then written up by your writing rules. The writing step runs on AssemblyAI's LLM Gateway, which the free credit doesn't cover, so it needs a small balance on your AssemblyAI account. Without one, dictations go in as heard, tidied, and the pill says so.
- New: a Language setting under Dictation. English is the default. STT-3.7's 32 languages are transcribed as you speak; languages outside them, such as Tamil, use Universal-2, which transcribes after you stop and takes a few seconds longer.
- Meetings are now always transcribed by AssemblyAI. If you had set Meetings to On device, your meeting audio now goes to AssemblyAI with your key. The on-device choice named people and placed their words too poorly on real meetings. A meeting recorded while AssemblyAI can't be reached (offline, or no key yet) keeps its audio and is transcribed once AssemblyAI can be reached.

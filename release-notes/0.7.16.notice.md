- If you loaded the VoiceiQ Meetings extension in Chrome, remove it yourself: open chrome://extensions, find VoiceiQ Meetings, and click Remove. VoiceiQ no longer uses it, and updating the app does not remove it.
- The on-device writer is gone. If "Writing rules and notes" was set to On device, it is now Off: your on-device dictations go in as heard, with your Dictionary's corrections. Choose Gemini there to have them written up. The downloaded writing model is deleted to free the space.
